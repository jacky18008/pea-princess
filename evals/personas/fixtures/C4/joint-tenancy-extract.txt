TENANCY AGREEMENT (extract sent by the agent, 2026-09-04)

Clause 3.1 The Tenants are jointly and severally liable for the whole of the Rent
and for any damage to the Property. Where one Tenant does not pay, the remaining
Tenants are liable for the shortfall.

Clause 3.4 The Tenancy is a single agreement for the whole Property. A Tenant who
leaves before the end of the term remains liable until a replacement is accepted
in writing by the Landlord.
