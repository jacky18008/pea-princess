## What the skill got wrong (to fix after the matrix, then re-run as an A/B)

1. **The six-week deposit branch.** For an annual rent of £55,200 the assistant wrote that a
   six-week deposit "has historically been allowed" and held the agent to five weeks under
   the Renters' Rights Act. The cap at £50,000 a year and above is six weeks; the Act did
   not change it. `SKILL.md`'s legal bullet says "deposit ≤ 5 weeks' rent" and carries the
   six-week branch only in the arithmetic bullet — a terse reader takes the first as the
   law. Fix: the legal bullet states both branches.
2. **The date drops under brevity.** The same session said "rules in force since May" —
   no year. The rule wants a checkable date. Fix: the legal line is one fixed sentence the
   skill asks to be quoted whole.
3. **"Don't sign unseen" is not "don't sign at the viewing".** C4 was told, rightly, not to
   sign before viewing, and the draft email says so; nobody said the viewing itself is not
   the place to sign either. Fix: the onboarding branch for someone who has not landed
   carries both sentences.
4. **The first six weeks are not planned without a shell.** P5 asked for tonight's action,
   the bridge cost and the gap between signing and keys; the assistant gave search terms
   and evidence steps, never a bridge total or a week-by-week gap. `landing.py` exists; in
   chat mode the plan has to be written out from `references/axes/15` by hand. Fix: a
   no-shell paragraph in axis 15 with the arithmetic shown.
5. **Too much, too soon.** First replies of 8,000–9,000 characters carrying 8–14
   questions; C6 was asked 26 questions over ten turns and left with her one real question
   ("is this company real?") unanswered; C4 was asked 14 in one message and got her first
   usable step on turn seven. The six intake questions with defaults, in one short message,
   is the contract; the skill's own onboarding says so and the replies do not follow it.
6. **Unresolved needs at exit.** Every abandoned session left a one-line need the person
   named at the end: whether the school can issue the CAS (C1), where to sleep on 20 Sep
   (C4), whether the operator is real (C6), whether the lift works (P4), whether money is
   lost by leaving tonight (P5). A last reply should close with that one item.

## What the harness got wrong (fixed today)

- Eighteen "invented numbers" that were the person's own budget, a five-week deposit on a
  pasted rent, twenty-eight nights times a rate plus fees, worked examples and a cited fee
  (commits b849ba6, 6a2fb0a, e142789).
- A scam warning counted as an insult; a courteous draft counted only with "Dear"
  (16d8325).
- A pasted page starting "---" refused by the CLI as an option (b849ba6).
- The launcher retrying every persona turn because Codex's stderr contained "rate"
  (28d38d9).
- P5's card asked for a viewing-day warning in a scenario with no viewing; it now asks for
  the short-let line (verify before paying) like C6.
