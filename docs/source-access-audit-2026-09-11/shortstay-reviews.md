# Short-stay and review source access audit

Checked: **2026-09-11**. Scope: Airbnb, Booking.com, Expedia, Premier Inn, Travelodge, HomeViews, Trustpilot and Google Maps/Places. The product is a permanently free local skill using each user's own agent, without a hosted worker, shared scraped database, payments or a provider-owned model. This review made no accounts, contacts, bookings, API calls or listing crawls. It reviewed public official policy and developer documentation only. No source text is quoted verbatim.

**No reviewed provider supplies an established, universally available, account-free and payment-free licence for this skill to automatically collect and retain arbitrary listings or reviews.** That is a finding about the checked documentation, not proof that no negotiated permission could exist. A free/local product does not itself establish a noncommercial-use exception; Booking.com expressly addresses noncommercial automation.

## Implications for the local skill

1. Separate **human handoff** from **licensed machine read** in the UI. Opening a native page can eliminate copy/paste for the human, but cannot honestly be labelled an automatically verified comparison. A user command, public accessibility, a browser login, robots rules or a search-result snippet does not establish the provider's downstream content licence.
2. Keep credentials out of the distributed skill. Partner APIs are conditional possibilities, not recommendations to obtain accounts, pay or negotiate now. No accounts, purchases or integrations were authorised or enabled in this audit. Permanent free distribution does not prevent users from separately choosing their own eligible paid API; any such optional integration must make its costs and terms explicit. Official API existence also does not establish that each install may share the developer's access.
3. Access and evidence retention need separate checks. Expedia Rapid reviews allow at most 48-hour caching; Trustpilot requires daily refresh of displayed reviews and deletion handling; Places generally prohibits storing content beyond explicit exceptions such as place IDs. A permanent raw-review journal is therefore not a universal lawful storage design. Provider-specific licensing must determine whether to retain text, expire it or retain only an allowed identifier and retrieval metadata. [Rapid reviews](https://developers.expediagroup.com/rapid/lodging/content/guest-reviews), [Trustpilot Data Solutions](https://developers.trustpilot.com/data-solutions-get-started), [Trustpilot caching](https://developers.trustpilot.com/ds-caching-best-practices/), [Places policies](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/policies).
4. Photos and authored reviews require their own display rights and attribution. API search permission alone is not permission to redistribute a photo archive or send content to any model. Google's current platform terms restrict copying, derivative uses and model training; a training prohibition alone does not decide whether every one-off inference is permitted. Assess the full use rather than silently equating the two. [Google Maps Platform terms §3.2.3](https://cloud.google.com/maps-platform/terms).
5. No generally licensed alert, brochure or export feed for arbitrary market inventory was established for these eight providers. Airbnb wishlists and Maps URLs are documented native handoffs. An export of a user's own bookings/account data would have a different scope from a licence to collect all available listings. Missing documentation is recorded as unknown, not as either consent or a proven prohibition.

Operation-by-operation statuses, clause locators, eligibility and uncertainties are in [shortstay-reviews.json](shortstay-reviews.json). These are evidence-based product routing decisions, not a determination of contract enforceability or statutory exceptions. UK-facing terms were preferred; Google billing-account location can change the applicable agreement. Recheck policy and admission status before enabling any future integration.
