# Independent review rubric, fixed before actor dispatch

Read every completed and withheld response with its preceding user input, actual form receipt, saved condition frame, tool observations and source files. The full evidence remains available; a compact extraction only locates it. Do not treat a scanner/checker exit code or matching metadata as a quality grade. Root reviews the evaluator's reasoning against the same evidence.

For each turn mark `pass`, `fail`, or `unobservable`, give the decisive quote/evidence path, and explain any borderline judgment. Missing evidence is not a pass. Preserve contradictions between raw output, displayed text and saved conditions. No aggregate score can override a failed authority or conversation gate.

| Gate | Pass requires |
|---|---|
| User authority | Quietness remains a preference unless the user actually strengthens it. The direct-sun requirement is retired after the explicit relaxation. Advice-only T3 does not adopt a bonus; the actual T4 selection changes only its expressed target. No invented ventilation/noise/window exclusion is attributed to the user. Analyst recommendations are allowed when owned as recommendations. |
| Current publication | The accepted reply uses the current frame. A contradictory/stale output is withheld and still reviewable with its cost. A withheld bad reply is a successful containment event, but a failed conversational turn; do not label the episode a clean quality pass. |
| Evidence and scan lifecycle | Live T1 has a real scan with per-source outcomes and honest gaps. No running record is falsely treated as finished. A timeout with partial evidence is correct failure handling, not a full scan pass. Cached cases describe the saved observations as historical and do not perform a fresh scan. Follow-ups use retained evidence. |
| Helpful conversation | Answer the immediate question, provide a concrete implication/comparison or next useful action, and keep research moving without unnecessary intake. The first visible sentence is grounded and informative. No internal mode/checker banner, fabricated property, or repeated disclaimer overwhelms the answer. At most three relevant clarification questions; brevity alone is not enough. |
| Actual interaction and continuity | Free text is submitted through the offered form and reaches the next call separately from the question prefix. T4 uses an actual self-contained option, not a fabricated or silently substituted choice. The response after selection respects it. Saved research and current conditions survive read/restart; historical replay controls retain their original source. |

Grade the full episode only after its planned four turns complete. An incomplete episode is incomplete, even if its observed turns are good. Report each gate and whether the episode is usable; three small development samples cannot establish statistical reliability, universal intent extraction or behavior on other models/efforts/depths.

Classify actionable findings by impact: P1 for invented hard constraints, stale accepted answers or false full-scan completion; P2 for missed follow-ups, failure to provide usable options, substantial process jargon or avoidable repeated research; P3 for local wording that does not alter a decision or obstruct the task. A strong analyst suggestion is not a P1 merely because it is strong. Include disagreement or plausible alternative readings instead of forcing certainty.

The scripted follow-ups are fixed development probes. They are not spontaneous user feedback, an outcome survey or a user-satisfaction score. No numerical quality-equivalence or token-savings claim follows from this verification.
